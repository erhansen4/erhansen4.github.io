Associate Professor of Political Science
Loyola University Chicago

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Political Science at Loyola University Chicago. I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill in 2017.

I conduct research on the background experiences of public officials, representation, and policymaking in state legislatures and Congress. I also study the relationship between educational attainment and voting.
